Does the demo use my camera?

No. The illustrated scene and example files are generated only inside this page. No camera, microphone or file access is requested.

Is the illustration actual camera output?

No. Request original photo and video files from the final sample. Check resolution, frame rate, recording conditions and image quality.

How do photos and clips reach the phone?

The supplier sheet lists Wi-Fi transfer. Confirm the selected app, first-time setup, phone compatibility and file handling.

Which model will I receive?

Confirm the final SKU before ordering. The current image folder is E03, the specification draft says ID215, and the earlier site used E09.

Are AI and translation included?

These functions are listed in the supplier draft as dependent on app connectivity. Confirm regional availability, supported languages, account requirements and service fees.
