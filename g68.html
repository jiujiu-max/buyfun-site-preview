The supplied specification sheet says built-in GPS is not supported. Any phone-based route function must be checked with the selected app and phone.

Which app is supplied?

The latest W02 sheet lists Fitnessx, while the earlier G68 sheet lists QWatch Pro. Confirm the final model, app, regional availability and fees before ordering.
