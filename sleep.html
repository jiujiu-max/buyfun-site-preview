06 / BUYER QUESTIONS

A clearer view.
A better decision.

Does the audio demo play real sound?

Yes. Tap play to hear an original music sample or synthesized rain ambience. These are website listening samples, not recordings through the mask. Evaluate the actual speaker experience on a sample.

Can users choose their own audio?

The proposed experience uses audio from a compatible phone. Verify phone pairing, playback and the physical controls with the selected model.

Will it fit side sleepers?

Comfort depends on the wearer, sleeping position and speaker placement. Test actual samples with the intended users.

Does it monitor sleep?

The supplier sheet lists sleep and other wellness records. Confirm which features are available, the final app and data presentation. The page shows no real measurements.

Is the mask washable?

Cleaning instructions and which parts can be removed must be confirmed with the supplier. Do not assume the electronic assembly is washable.
